Hint: use the page B practice to find tₐ mentally.

Maximum height is a special case of finding the displacement when u, v and the time interval are known: here v = 0 and the time interval is tₐ.

Hmax = ½ (u + 0) tₐ = ½ u tₐ

An object is projected up with a speed of 20 m/s from the top of a tower 30 m high. What is the maximum height reached above the ground?

50 m. Hmax = ½ × 20 × 2 = 20 m above the top of the tower. Height above the ground = Hmax + height of the tower = 20 + 30 = 50 m.

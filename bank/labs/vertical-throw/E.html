For u = 30 m/s, calculate v at t = 0 and then at each successive second. Then visualize the shape of the v–t graph.

Click +1 s to see the graph take shape.

The change in velocity in each second equals the average acceleration over that second.

If the acceleration is constant, the change is the same every second. So the points lie on a straight line, and its slope equals the acceleration.

slope = Δv/Δt = −10 m/s ÷ 1 s = −10 m/s² = a

For u = 25 m/s, at what time will the v–t graph cross the time axis?

At t = 2.5 s. The graph crosses the time axis where v = 0, which is the top: tₐ = 25 ÷ 10 = 2.5 s.

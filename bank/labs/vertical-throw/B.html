Observe and practiceCalculate tₐ for u = 25 m/s, then click ▶ Play to verify your answer. Repeat for u = 30 m/s and 35 m/s.

u (m/s)

The time of ascent is the time taken for the velocity v to become zero: the ball stops moving up.

Notice how this relates to page A: v = 35 − 10t becomes 0 at t = 3.5 s.

Using the formula v = u + at, with v = 0 at the top and a = −10 m/s²:

0 = u − 10tₐ  ⇒  tₐ = u/10

Is the same tₐ valid if the particle is projected from a height?

Yes. The time of ascent depends only on u, not on the height of projection. When projected from a height, the time of descent is greater than the time of ascent.

Learning goal CCalculate the displacement in your head from the velocities at the start and end of a time interval.
Observe and practice

Displacement = mean velocity × time interval (uniformly accelerated motion).

Calculate the displacement, then verify by clicking the matching button:

  1. 1) t = 0 to 3 s
  2. 2) t = 0 to 3.5 s
  3. 3) t = 0 to 4 s
  4. 4) t = 3 to 5 s

Hint: use the page A practice to find v mentally.

In uniformly accelerated motion, the average velocity over an interval is the mean of the velocities at its start and end. So:

s = ½ (u + v)(t₂ − t₁)

where u and v are the velocities at t₁ and t₂.

Trap: always use a consistent sign convention for u and v (up is +). For 3 → 5 s: u = +5 m/s and v = −15 m/s, so s = ½ (+5 − 15) × 2 = −10 m.

When is the displacement equal to the distance travelled?

When the motion during the interval is in one direction only: u and v have the same sign (or one of them is zero), so the ball does not turn around in between.

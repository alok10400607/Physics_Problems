Vertical Throw Practice

G
Learning goal GFind the time of flight of an object thrown up from a height.
Observe and learn

A ball is thrown up at 30 m/s from a 35 m tower. Find the time it takes to hit the ground, without pen and paper. Then press ▶ Play, and Continue for the fall.

Hint: starting down at 30 m/s, how far does it go in 1 s?

The ball reaches the level of projection with a velocity of equal magnitude but opposite direction: v = −30 m/s, after 2tₐ = 6 s.

From there it falls 35 m, starting at 30 m/s. After 1 s its speed is 40 m/s, so the mean speed is 35 m/s and it covers 35 × 1 = 35 m. The last part takes exactly 1 s.

Time of flight = 6 s + 1 s = 7 s
Check: −35 = 30t − 5t² ⇒ t = 7 s

What will be the time of flight of an object projected up at 25 m/s from a height of 30 m?

6 s. It returns to the launch level after 2 × 2.5 = 5 s, moving down at 25 m/s. In the next 1 s its speed goes from 25 to 35 m/s: mean 30 m/s × 1 s = 30 m, exactly the height. Total 5 + 1 = 6 s.

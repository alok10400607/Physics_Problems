Learning goal HSee 2D projectile motion as two independent 1D motions.
Observe and learn

A ball is projected at u = 50 m/s at 53° to the horizontal, so uₓ = 30 m/s and uy = 40 m/s. Press ▶ Play or +1 s and watch its shadows on the two axes.

The 2D motion is two independent 1D motions.

Split u into components: uₓ = 50 cos 53° = 30 m/s and uy = 50 sin 53° = 40 m/s.

  • Horizontal: no acceleration, so the x-shadow moves at a steady 30 m/s, covering 30 m every second.
  • Vertical: a = −10 m/s², so the y-shadow moves exactly like a ball thrown straight up at 40 m/s (pages A–G).

The two motions are independent. They share only the time.

x = uₓ t = 30t
y = uy t − 5t² = 40t − 5t²

What will be the maximum height reached by the particle?

80 m. It is the same as the maximum height of a particle projected straight up with uy = 40 m/s: tₐ = 40 ÷ 10 = 4 s, so Hmax = ½ × 40 × 4 = 80 m.

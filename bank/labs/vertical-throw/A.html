Learning goal AFind the velocity at any instant by mental calculation.

Observe and practiceA ball is thrown up from the ground at u = 35 m/s. Before each click, find v at the next time in your head. Then click +0.5 s and check your answer on the graph.

v changes by −10 m/s every second, so by −5 m/s every half second.

After t seconds:

v = u − 10t = 35 − 10t

v positive: moving up. v = 0: the top. v negative: moving down.

For u = 35 m/s, after how many seconds will the ball be moving downwards with a speed of 10 m/s?

4.5 s. Moving down at 10 m/s means v = −10 m/s. From +35 m/s to −10 m/s, v must decrease by 45 m/s. At 10 m/s every second, that takes 4.5 s.
